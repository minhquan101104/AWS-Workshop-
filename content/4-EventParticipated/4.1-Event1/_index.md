# Summary Report: “Study Tour AWS - Enterprise Cloud Architectures & Industry Applications”

### Event Overview

* **Date:** Saturday, July 04, 2026.
* **Location:** Bitexco Financial Tower, Ho Chi Minh City.
* **Co-organizers:** Swinburne Vietnam, AWS Vietnam, Renova Cloud, and Cloud Kinetics.
* **Scale:** Over 100 outstanding students traveled long distances to attend alongside top industry experts and GenAI Engineers.

---

### Preparation & Student Engagement

A memorable journey featuring over 100 students starting as early as 5:00 AM, traveling over 200 km to Bitexco Financial Tower. Their proactive learning spirit and high energy created an extremely vibrant atmosphere during exchange sessions with industry leaders.

---

### Core Event Highlights

* **Global Cloud & AI Job Market Overview:** Updates on the latest recruitment trends in Cloud Computing, Generative AI (GenAI), and global career development roadmaps.
* **From Academia to Real-World Engineering:** Inspiring stories of transitioning from a university student to a professional Data Engineer, exploring how enterprises optimize data architectures and operate massive systems on AWS.
* **Bridging "Learning by Doing":** Secrets to transforming theoretical classroom knowledge into actionable technical solutions to impress top tech employers.
* **Inspiring Fireside Chat:** In-depth discussions on strategies for nurturing and empowering the next generation of breakthrough tech talents in Vietnam alongside representatives from AWS, Renova Cloud, and Cloud Kinetics.

---

### Key Takeaways

* **Problem-Solving Mindset:** Technologies and tools evolve constantly, but a strong analytical problem-solving mindset remains the sustainable golden key.
* **Proactive Hands-on Spirit:** Taking initiative in real-world technical execution is the decisive factor to stay ahead of industry trends and demonstrate capability.

---

### Personal Reflections & Practical Application

* **Career Direction Alignment:** Gained realistic enterprise perspectives that helped clearly define my roadmap toward becoming a Cloud Solutions Architect.
* **Technical Skill Enhancement:** Strengthened self-learning commitment, continuing to boost hands-on AWS lab practices (Serverless, Generative AI Agents) to meet rigorous industry recruitment standards.
