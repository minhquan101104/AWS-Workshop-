### 2. Core Contents & Technical Key Takeaways

#### Topic 1: Signal Scout – Detecting Strategic Corporate Change Signals (Team: Dream AI Team)
* **Business Challenge:** Risk management and corporate strategy teams face difficulties in aggregating and detecting early signals of corporate restructuring from fragmented public data sources[cite: 3].
* **Signal Scout Solution:** A platform that connects scattered signals into a comprehensive picture, delivering transparent analyses backed by cited evidence to support executives in making Maintain, Adapt, or Accelerate decisions[cite: 3].
* **Cost-Optimized Infrastructure Model:** A flexible AWS architecture leveraging Amazon Bedrock, AgentCore, S3, and DynamoDB to optimize actual operational costs based on usage workloads[cite: 3].

#### Topic 2: SA Professional AI-Native App – Automating the Solution Architect Workflow (Team: Plan V)
* **Status Quo & Pain Points:** Traditional cloud architecture workflows (reading BRDs/PRDs, manual diagramming, writing IaC, and estimating costs) are time-consuming and heavily dependent on personal experience[cite: 2].
* **AI-Native App Solution:** An application designed to assist Solution Architects by extracting requirements from natural language documents, automatically generating official AWS-compliant diagrams (Draw.io MCP), producing Terraform code (IaC), and estimating cloud operating costs[cite: 2].
* **Architecture & Cloud Infrastructure:** Utilizes Serverless and Containerized models on AWS (CloudFront, Cognito, Fargate, Amazon Bedrock, EFS, S3, and RDS PostgreSQL) to ensure high availability and security[cite: 2].

#### Topic 3: AI-Powered Conversation Ordering – Ordering via Conversational AI (Team: One Team)
* **Generative AI in E-Commerce:** Optimizes customer shopping and ordering experiences through intuitive natural language interaction interfaces.
* **Conversational Processing & Context:** Applies Large Language Models (LLMs) for Intent Recognition, order status management, and real-time product data querying.
* **System Integration:** Seamlessly connects Conversational AI flows with Backend services and payment/shipping APIs, enabling an automated, accurate, and smooth ordering process.

#### Topic 4: Hackathon Journey – 24 Hours of Building, Failing, and Learning (Team: 3KA)
* **Mindset & Real-World Challenges:** Embracing the hands-on spirit to overcome initial limitations in time and AI/AWS knowledge, while handling real-world issues (sleep deprivation, uncommitted code, workflow friction) during a 24-hour sprint[cite: 1].
* **S.H.E.P.H.E.R.D Solution:** Builds an automated camera video analysis system to monitor crowd density, evaluate queue conditions, and provide early warnings for congestion using YOLO, ByteTrack, and Amazon Bedrock Agent[cite: 1].
* **Key Takeaways:**
    * **Scope it tiny:** Focus on delivering a fully functional core MVP feature rather than overcommitting to an ambitious, unfinished idea[cite: 1].
    * **Thorough preparation:** Prepare essential toolkits (starter templates), establish clear team roles (Code, Design, Pitch), and rehearse the 3-minute pitch story[cite: 1].
